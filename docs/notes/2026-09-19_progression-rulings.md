# Progression rulings from Anthony — 2026-09-19 (for the Design Chat to lock)

*Written down by Claude Code from the conversation that followed the baseline (`docs/balance/2026-09-19_baseline/FINDINGS.md`). These are Anthony's words turned into statements; the numbers marked (proposed) are Claude Code's reading and are his to change. A tuning proposal measured against them is in `docs/balance/2026-09-19_proposal/`.*

## Ruled

1. **Length.** A player at 3–4 hours a day takes the whole mountain in 2–3 months; about 2 months on the most efficient route (~210 hours of play). The game is built for longevity: past the mountain comes gear grinding, levelling more Adventurers, and events.
2. **Nobody steady is six months in without reaching the dragon.** A 2-hour-a-day player may take noticeably longer than 2–3 months, and that is acceptable, but not ten months. (proposed: ~300 hours ≈ 5 months at 2 h/day.)
3. **Ten walls.** The nine area bosses, then the dragon (Vaelyx) above the King as a tenth wall.
4. **Every boss is a wall.** No area is walked through.
5. **Every area takes about the same play time**, with areas 1–3 about 20 % shorter than the rest. (proposed for 210 h: 17.9 h each for areas 1–3, 22.3 h each for areas 4–10 — about a week per area at 3.5 h/day.)
6. **The reference feel is Eternal Hero's per-world grind** — a wall you grind up to over weeks. (Tension noted: weeks per world × ten walls is 6–9 months, not 2–3. Proposed resolution: keep ~210 h for the mountain and put the longer grind into the post-dragon tail.)
7. **Healing in a fight is minimal.** A party that is mid-geared for the area loses life in every fight. That is what makes sanctuaries useful. (proposed: a pack fight costs 10–15 % of party health; a fight heals back no more than about a third of what it takes.)
8. **XP is a speed control.** Levels are paced deliberately through the XP curve. (proposed: ~4.5 levels per area, the dragon met near level 45, level 50 in the tail.)
9. **Replaying zones is the game.** People grind the same areas over and over to climb slowly. That loop is the main gameplay.
10. **Gems must be much rarer**, and the acquisition needs another strategy. Leaning: **shards that craft a gem of your own choice**, so pacing is controllable. Not decided; excluded from the tuning test.

## Open — Anthony asked, not yet ruled

- **What keeps people playing after the dragon?** Claude Code's suggestions, in order of value: (a) each area stays relevant — its own materials and enemy traits that favour certain archetypes, so a broad roster has a purpose; (b) an endless descent on the same run loop, measured by the deepest floor reached; (c) deeper versions of cleared areas with item-tier-7 drops (the code already maps a tenth area to tier 7); (d) calendar events; (e) a collection book for the 224 weapon names and the boss weapons. Already in the game and sized for a long tail: Resonance ranks VI–X (need ten or more levelled Adventurers), four class gems per role via shards, Artifact / Mythic grades from Cores, level 50.
- **What breaks a wall.** Today: levels (+5 % each), weapon rarity / rating / empower within the area's item tier, armor (only from Hearth time), Bond upgrades. Claude Code's concern: if armor stays Hearth-only, walls are timed by waiting rather than by playing — suggest armor materials also drop from fights.
- **Worldvein sinks.** The baseline spends 1.4 % of what it earns. Not addressed by the tuning pass.

## Not to be contradicted by any of the above

No pay-to-win, no gacha power, no prestige / reset mechanics, no stat rerolls (design doc NEVER list). A harder version of an area or an endless mode takes nothing away from the player, so it is not a reset.
