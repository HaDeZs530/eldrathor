# Eldrathor — Zone Overview & Worldvein Lore

> Source: `eldrathor_overview.txt` (OneDrive\Stormhalter Worlds\HaDeZs Test), converted to Markdown.

## The World

A coastal island with a mountain at its heart. A harbor town sits at the southeast shore. From there, players climb up the mountain through five levels to a ruined castle at the summit, where an ancient dragon resides.

## The Lore

A magical energy source called the **Worldvein** runs through the mountain — raw geological mana, manifesting as blue crystal. Centuries ago, a colony called the **People of the Vein** settled the island and harnessed the Worldvein to forge powerful weapons and armor. Industrial overuse fractured the master crystal (the **Worldforge**), and raw magic began bleeding into the land — empowering all the wildlife, making the island dangerous.

A green-scaled dragon, **Vaelyx the Eternal**, was drawn from across the world by the leak (his kind feeds on raw magic). The colony fought him for three days with their full arsenal. They lost. The king, queen, and royal court died at the foot of the Worldforge, bound by its energy as spirit-guardians. Vaelyx arranged them across the mountain as his "court" — pawns and toys.

A century has passed. The descendants of the colony still live in the harbor town but cannot climb the mountain. The leak keeps spreading. Players are the heroes who hold the line.

## The Five Levels

- **L1 — Harbor Town:** the surviving descendants' home. Quest hub, beach, foothills.
- **L2 — Ruined Seaside Town:** the colony's second tier, fallen and overgrown with corruption.
- **L3 — Serpent's Spire:** dangerous multi-level bridge maze across jagged mountain peaks. Fall off and you're "rescued" by very confused Brooklyn-accent mermaids (who are actually tentacled horrors that think they're beautiful).
- **L4 — Temple Forge:** two-floor forge built into the mountain where the colony made their weapons. Four bound court bosses inside.
- **L5 — Castle / Dragon's Lair:** the summit. King and Queen in side boss rooms, then Vaelyx in the throne room above.

## The Hook

Players forge their own builds using a gem-based enchantment system fed by the corrupted mobs throughout the zone. It's a recognizable high-fantasy zone — coast, mountain, dragon — that contrasts with the shard's other zones.
