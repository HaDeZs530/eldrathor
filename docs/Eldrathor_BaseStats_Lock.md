# Base Stat Spine (§6a-stats) — LOCKED July 2026

*Designed and locked by Anthony + Claude Design Chat, 2026-07-19. Committed to the repo 2026-09-11 (was previously only in chat). Referenced by the Class Gem Trees lock, archetype seeds, weapons, armor, and the Veinbinder Bond tree.*

The universal stat vocabulary. **Every system references these nine stats and nothing else** — archetype spines, weapon stats, armor, empowerment, gem tree headers, and the Veinbinder Bond tree all grant, scale, or read from this list. A single shared vocabulary keeps character sheets legible and lets every tree/screen show honest cumulative totals (no hidden math).

**Unified damage (from §6a):** there is no Strength/Agility/Intelligence split — that would rebuild the physical/magic divide the "all damage is Mythros" lock removed. A single **Power** stat scales all Mythros damage regardless of weapon or archetype.

## The nine base stats

| # | Stat | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **HP** | Survivability pool. |
| 2 | **Mana** | Resource spent on abilities. *(Working placeholder name — "Mythros" is a candidate.)* |
| 3 | **Mana Regen** | Rate the Mana pool refills; sets ability/fight tempo. |
| 4 | **Power** | Unified Mythros damage scaler. Replaces Str/Agi/Int; scales ALL damage regardless of weapon. |
| 5 | **Worldvein Mitigation** | Damage resist (single unified resist — no elemental/damage-type split). |
| 6 | **Attack Speed** | How often a character acts (attacks + ability cadence). |
| 7 | **Crit Chance** | Chance to critically strike — the swinginess the combat spec (§8c) requires. |
| 8 | **Crit Damage** | Critical-hit damage multiplier. |
| 9 | **Healing Power** | Scales all healing output. |

## Class-flavor stats (extend the base nine)
- **Taunt Strength** (Tank gem) — magnitude/duration of taunt focus-pull.
- **Control Strength** (Controller gem) — magnitude/duration of enemy-action suppression (stun / slow / weaken / vulnerability).

*(Healer needs no flavor stat — Healing Power is in the base nine. DPS needs none — Power/Crit cover it.)*

## Deliberate exclusions (LOCKED — do NOT add)
- **No accuracy / evasion / hit-chance.** A miss layer fights the "watch it auto-resolve" feel — every action lands and is visible.
- **No separate armor/resist types.** ONE mitigation stat, never fire/ice/etc.
- **No Strength/Agility/Intelligence.** Collapsed into Power.

## Stat sources (no overlap)
- **Archetype seeds** — flat base numbers per archetype (see `Eldrathor_Archetype_Seeds_DRAFT.md`; numbers under review).
- **Power** — primarily driven by weapon skill level (§6c), plus gear.
- **Armor** — crafted stat contribution + socketed armor gems (passive).
- **Class gem tree** — its slice of these stats, as % multipliers on the seeds.
- **Veinbinder Bond tree** — permanent party-wide stat boosts (§6f).
